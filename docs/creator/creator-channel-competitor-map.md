# Executive Summary

This document is a dated snapshot of who is making Project Zomboid video
content on YouTube as of 2026-10-07, what each channel is doing with the
game, and where the observable catalogue is thin. Nine channels were opened
first-hand and are profiled: ambiguousamphibian, Pr1vateLime, NurseVO,
Retanaru, Mattsi, MrAtomicDuck, Awpenheimer, ZiubisPZ and Meyhem Gaming
Plays [4][5][6][7][8][9][10][11][12]. For each, the channel page and the
public upload feed were read on the retrieval date, so the figures below are
"as displayed on 2026-10-07", never creator analytics.

The headline observations are narrow and countable. Two of the biggest
channels in the sample keep Project Zomboid as only part of their slate:
ambiguousamphibian's last fifteen uploads include one Project Zomboid title
[4], and Pr1vateLime's include eight [5]. NurseVO's last fifteen are almost
entirely Project Zomboid streams [6]. Retanaru kept analysing the unstable
branch in its titles until June 2026 and now titles videos against plain
"B42" [7]. Mattsi's most recent upload is dated 2026-02-06 [8].

Build 42 stable has moved on since the creator track's foundation was
written: 42.21 reached stable on 2026-09-28 [1], so stable-branch B42 footage
dated before that predates the 42.21 changes, which include zombie-persistence
fixes, XXL-tree cutaway changes and Welder starting-recipe changes [3][17]. Document confidence is **Medium**. The facts
are first-hand and dated, but they are a sample (the most recent fifteen
uploads per channel plus four search probes), the search probes are ranked by
YouTube's own algorithm, and the Twitch layer of the landscape could not be
opened at all.

# Key Takeaways

- Nine channels were opened and profiled with dated upload evidence; the
  displayed subscriber counts range from 4.83 thousand to 1.68 million
  *(cited, as displayed 2026-10-07)* [4][5][6][7][8][9][10][11].
- Project Zomboid share of recent output differs sharply by channel:
  1 of 15 titles for ambiguousamphibian, 8 of 15 for Pr1vateLime, 14 of 15
  for NurseVO *(cited, title counts)* [4][5][6].
- Retanaru is the only profiled channel whose recent titles are dominated by
  patch-by-patch mechanics analysis, and its titles dropped the word
  "Unstable" in the two most recent uploads *(cited)* [7].
- Mattsi's last upload is dated 2026-02-06, eight months before the
  retrieval date *(cited)* [8].
- B42 stable is 42.21 as of 2026-09-28; the preceding stable was 42.20.0
  (2026-07-29) with hotfixes, so stable footage dated before 2026-09-28 is
  pre-42.21 *(cited)* *(B42)* [1][2][17].
- The four topic probes (modding, dedicated servers, lore, B41-to-B42
  changes) each returned a populated result page, so "gap" here means thin
  recency on the current build, not absence *(cited, search snapshot)*
  [13][14][15][16].
- No Twitch channel page was opened; Twitch coverage is not mapped
  *(cited limitation)*.

# Purpose

A creator planning a Project Zomboid channel, or a KB maintainer deciding
which documents give a creator the most to work with, needs to know who is
already publishing, in what format, how often, and on which game build. This
document supplies that map. It deliberately stops at observation; the
recommendations live in Practical Guidance and are labelled as judgement.

# Scope

Covered: YouTube channels that were opened on 2026-10-07; the format each
publishes as evidenced by recent upload titles; observed upload dates;
whether titles reference B42 stable; and four search-result probes used to
test topic coverage. Subscriber and view figures appear only as displayed by
YouTube on the retrieval date.

Not covered: Twitch, Kick and other live platforms (not opened); channel
revenue or analytics; private individuals (only public channel names are
used); judgements about quality; and game mechanics, which belong to the
Players and Modders tracks. The channel sample is not exhaustive; channels
absent from this document are simply not verified here. For the format
taxonomy and launch-window analysis, go to creator-foundation rather than
re-reading it here.

# Definitions

- **Upload feed** — the public per-channel feed YouTube serves at
  `youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml`, listing the most recent uploads with
  publish date and a view counter [4].
- **Displayed figure** — a subscriber, video or view number exactly as the
  YouTube page or feed showed it on 2026-10-07; platform-rounded and not
  analytics [4].
- **Title share** — the count of a channel's last fifteen feed titles that
  name Project Zomboid, a derived count that does not prove which build a
  video was recorded on.
- **Search probe** — one YouTube search query run on 2026-10-07 with the
  first eight results noted by uploader and displayed relative age
  [13][14][15][16].
- **Unstable / stable** — the opt-in test branch and the public default
  branch of the game respectively [1][3].

## Observed cadence and Project Zomboid share

- **ambiguousamphibian.** The fifteen newest feed entries span 2026-06-03 to
  2026-10-02, with a median gap of roughly a week between uploads. Exactly
  one title names Project Zomboid: "How Long Can I Survive a FLOODED World in
  Project Zomboid?", dated 2026-06-26, which predates the 42.20 stable
  release of 2026-07-29 [2][4]. The other fourteen titles name other games,
  including Stellaris, RimWorld and Kenshi [4].
- **Pr1vateLime.** The fifteen newest entries span 2026-08-08 to 2026-10-05.
  Eight name Project Zomboid, six are an episodic series in a space-trading
  game, and one covers another title [5]. The Project Zomboid titles include
  a five-part "28 Minutes Later Challenge" run (2026-09-02 to 2026-09-22), a
  Necroa-virus supercut (2026-08-11), a mod survival video (2026-08-14) and a
  limb-loss challenge (2026-09-30) [5]. The cadence is roughly one upload
  every three to five days [5].
- **NurseVO.** The fifteen newest entries span 2026-09-14 to 2026-10-07,
  almost one per day. Fourteen of the fifteen titles carry the "#LIVE" tag
  and name Project Zomboid [6]. Topics in the titles include an NPC
  simulation mod, an extraction-style mode, a "Viewpoint" mode and a
  random-server join [6].
- **Retanaru.** The fifteen newest entries span 2025-10-20 to 2026-09-27, an
  irregular cadence with gaps of about two months in places (2026-06-23 to
  2026-08-24) [7]. Titles through 2026-06-23 explicitly say "Unstable"; the
  2026-08-24 guns video and the 2026-09-27 vision video are titled against
  "B42" without that word [7].
- **Mattsi.** The fifteen newest entries span 2025-09-23 to 2026-02-06. Thirteen
  are dated between 2025-09-23 and 2025-10-31, then single uploads follow on
  2025-11-05 and 2026-02-06 [8]. The 2025-11-05 animal guide and 2025-10-11
  first-month guide are the two entries with the highest displayed views in
  that window (56,172 and 104,555) [8].
- **MrAtomicDuck.** The eight newest entries span 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-30
  and include a news video on the unstable branch returning (2026-09-27)
  and a moodle explainer (2026-09-30) [9].
- **Awpenheimer.** The newest entry (2026-09-26) is a video on the 42.21
  update; earlier entries cover a first-person mod and a multiplayer

- **ZiubisPZ.** Eight entries across 2026-09-15 to 2026-10-05, mostly mod

Awpenheimer's 42.21 update video (2026-09-26) [10], Meyhem Gaming Plays'
B42.21 video (2026-09-26) [12], ZiubisPZ's "Build 42 Stable" base locations

changes, a Welder-occupation starting-recipe change and map and UI updates, so
a "what's new in 42.21" video can be checked against a primary list [17].

# Practical Guidance

These are judgements built on the observations above. They introduce no new
facts.

## Reading the competitive picture

- The channels that appear to compete most directly for a new creator's
  attention in the current-build space are those whose recent titles are
  mostly Project Zomboid: NurseVO for live play [6], Pr1vateLime for
  challenge series [5], and Retanaru for mechanics [7]. The very large
  ambiguousamphibian channel currently publishes little Project Zomboid [4],
  so its subscriber count says little about direct competition for B42
  search traffic.
- MrAtomicDuck, Awpenheimer, ZiubisPZ and Meyhem Gaming Plays were all
  publishing build-news or mod-list content within days of the 42.21 stable
  date [9][10][11][12]. That slot (update coverage within a week) is
  populated; a new entrant is unlikely to win it on speed alone.
- Treat displayed views as relative signals within one channel's own
  catalogue, not as targets. Mattsi's two highest-viewed entries in the
  sample are long-form guides, while its short clips sit near or under a few
  thousand views [8]; that supports, but does not prove, a guides-over-clips
  conclusion for that channel.

## Gap candidates the KB can fact-support

Each candidate pairs an observation with a KB document that can supply
verified facts. They are candidates, not proven under-served topics.

1. **Current-build transition content.** Six of eight transition-query
   results were about a year old [16]. A video built from
   players-b41-to-b42-transition and dated to 42.21 would be among the
   freshest results for that query.
2. **Verified beginner material.** Mattsi's high-view beginner guides date
   from autumn 2025 [8], well before the 2026-07-29 stable release [2].
   players-beginner-guide-b42 gives a fact base for a refresh.
3. **Modded-server operations.** The server-setup probe returned
   hosting-company and setup tutorials aged two to nine months [14]; none of
   the eight titles, as displayed, mentions mods. admins-modded-server-runbook supports that angle.
4. **Mod-making for current stable.** Only one tutorial-style result in the
   modding probe was a month old or newer [13], while mod-list videos were the
   freshest entries. modders-first-mod-tutorial-b42 gives a verified base.
5. **Lore.** The lore probe is already well supplied with recent uploads
   [15]; this is a weak gap candidate and probably best left to creators
   with a narrative voice.

# Common Pitfalls & Troubleshooting

- **Quoting a subscriber count without a date.** The figures here are
  displayed values for one day [4][5][6][7][8][9][10][11]; reuse them only
  with that date.
- **Inferring build from a title.** "Build 42" in a title does not give a
  point release [7][10][12]. Check the video itself before treating it as
  42.20- or 42.21-accurate.
- **Counting a channel's size as Project Zomboid reach.** The largest
  channel in the sample published one Project Zomboid title in its last
  fifteen uploads [4].
- **Treating a search probe as a census.** The probes list eight results
  each [13][14][15][16]; topics can have far more coverage below those
  eight.
- **Assuming the map is complete.** Only channels opened on 2026-10-07 are
  listed; omission is not a statement about any other channel.

# Community Notes & Unverified Claims

## Claim 1 — The large PZ creators also run their main audience on Twitch

- **Claim:** Community discussion and stat-site listings associate several of
  these creators, notably NurseVO, with Project Zomboid live streaming on
  Twitch in addition to YouTube.
- **Why unverified:** No Twitch channel page was opened during this research,
  so Twitch presence, cadence and viewership are unobserved. Only YouTube
  live-tagged uploads were seen [6].
- **Confidence:** Low. The YouTube "#LIVE" tags in [6] are consistent with
  live play but say nothing about the Twitch platform.

## Claim 2 — Long-form guides outperform short clips for evergreen search

- **Claim:** Creators commonly say that long beginner guides keep earning
  views for months while short clips spike and fade.
- **Why unverified:** The view counters in the feeds [8] are single-day
  snapshots with no time series, so velocity and decay cannot be measured.
- **Confidence:** Low. One channel's top-viewed entries [8] fit the claim,
  but a single channel and a single snapshot is thin support.

## Claim 3 — Update-news slots are saturated within days of a stable release

- **Claim:** Creators say the first week after a stable release is crowded
  with news videos, making late arrivals invisible.
- **Why unverified:** Four channels were seen publishing around 42.21 [9][10][11][12],
  but how many others did, and how they ranked, was not measured.
- **Confidence:** Low. The observed four are an unknown fraction of the real
  total.

# Risks & Caveats

- **Sampling.** Fifteen uploads per channel can miss a series, hiatus or
  format change that sits just outside the window [4][5][6][7][8].
- **Counter semantics.** Feed view counters and channel-page figures are
  YouTube-rounded and refresh on YouTube's schedule; counts for fresh
  uploads will be low relative to what they later become [6][12].
- **Pr1vateLime and MrAtomicDuck video totals** were not captured from the
  page text; only subscriber counts are shown [5][9].
- **Meyhem Gaming Plays** displayed no subscriber figure in the captured
  page text, so no scale is claimed [12].
- **Staleness.** Upload cadence changes within weeks. The review date of
  2027-01-05 is a soft deadline; a creator-facing decision should use a fresh
  pull.
- **Release context.** The game facts rest on the Steam announcements [1][2]
  [3] and the abridged 42.21 forum list [17]; hotfixes after 2026-09-28 may
  already have changed what a tutorial should say.

# Verification Steps

1. Open each channel's canonical page [4]–[12] and compare the displayed
   subscriber and video counts against the table.
2. Fetch each channel's upload feed (the `feeds/videos.xml` path on
   youtube.com, with the channel id from the cited canonical URL as its
   `channel_id` parameter) and recount the Project
   Zomboid titles among the newest fifteen.
3. Repeat the four search probes [13][14][15][16] and compare uploader and
   age distributions.
4. Confirm the 42.21 stable date and the unstable-first procedure in the
   Steam announcements [1][3], and the change list in the forum post [17].
5. For Claim 1, open each creator's Twitch page directly and record
   whatever it shows with a date.
